Privacy Policy

Karnataka Govt Exam — Master Study Tracker is designed as a local-first study application.

Data stored locally

Study progress, notes, priorities, revision information, doubts, mistakes, settings and other tracker data are stored locally on the user's device using browser/app storage. Study materials selected by the user are also stored locally when supported by the device/browser.

No automatic collection

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect location, contacts, call logs, SMS, microphone or camera data. It does not include advertising or analytics by default.

Ask ChatGPT

If the user chooses the Ask ChatGPT feature, the user is taken to ChatGPT. Information entered or intentionally shared with ChatGPT is handled according to ChatGPT's own privacy practices.

Deletion and backup

The app may provide local-data deletion and backup/restore features. Clearing app/browser storage or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data. Users should keep a backup of important information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change.
